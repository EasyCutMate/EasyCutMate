# EasyCutMate Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

This policy explains what the EasyCutMate Android app collects about you, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

## What we collect
When you create an account:
- **First and last name**
- **Email address** (we send you one email to confirm it)
- **Phone / WhatsApp number**
- **Your password**: handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.

While you use the app:
- An **anonymous phone ID**: a scrambled code made from your phone's Android ID, so your account works on one phone at a time. It can't be turned back into your phone's real ID.
- Your **account status**: free trial or full version, when your trial started, and your payment reference (e.g. `ECM-7K2P9X`).
- The **app version** you use and when you last opened the app.

We do **not** collect your videos, your YouTube links, your location, your contacts or anything else on your phone. Videos are edited on your phone and stay there.

## Why we collect it
- To run your free trial and your full version, and to keep your account to one phone at a time.
- To match your payment to your account when you buy the full version.
- To contact you about your account or purchase.

We don't sell your details, show you ads or share your details with anyone else.

## Where it's stored
Your details are stored with **Google Firebase** (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication), in Google's Singapore data centre. Google processes them for us under its own security and privacy terms.

## How long we keep it
As long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete it, we delete your details and account within 30 days.

## Your choices
You can ask us at any time to:
- see the details we hold about you,
- correct them, or
- **delete your account and details**.

To make a request, contact support at **iam1ofthedeveloper@gmail.com**. Please include your account email or payment reference so we can locate your account.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
